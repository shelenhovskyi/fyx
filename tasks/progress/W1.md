## Handoff

- Owns `terminal_ext` (leaf: OSC tap, ids/tokens, status, sanitiser), `terminal_host` (tab status, Agent
  Sessions panel, `ui_ext::terminal::TerminalHost`), planned `fyx_relay`, and the `fyx:` hooks in
  crates/terminal, terminal_view and zed's main.rs (`git grep -n 'fyx:' crates/terminal*`).
- `terminal_host::host(cx)` returns the `Arc<dyn TerminalHost>`. W3 (local_31b10de5) confirmed
  `ui_ext_host::set_terminal_host(Arc<dyn TerminalHost>, cx)`: a global read by later loads only. W3 builds it
  after abaac895a5 is on fyx/main and messages the commit; then call it in crates/zed/src/main.rs with
  `terminal_host::host(cx)` after `terminal_host::init` and before the first extension registry scan.
- Threading: `HostFront` checks, rate-limits and sanitises on the caller's thread, then queues
  `host::Command`s (Repaint, OpenPseudo, Output, Close) to the main-thread loop (`terminal_host::run`).
- Pseudo-terminals (P2.2): `TerminalExt::pseudo(ext, backend)` replaces the `fyx` field of a display-only
  `Terminal`. Upstream hooks: `write_to_pty` sends input to the backend, the Resize arm sends the size,
  `process_event` drops PtyWrite/ClipboardLoad/ClipboardStore/ColorRequest/TextAreaSizeRequest for pseudo
  terminals, and terminal_view's `persistence.rs` does not save them (else a restart turns them into shells).
  `sanitize.rs` re-encodes output through alacritty's vte and drops every sequence the terminal answers,
  DCS/APC/PM/SOS and most OSC. Input: 30 events/s, 64 KiB, 1 MiB buffer, last size only.
  Tabs open unfocused in the active window's terminal panel; `in_panel` terminals are held by the host
  and reached with `terminal_host::extension_terminal(ext, handle, cx)` (for W3's `terminal` node).
- Dev exe runs: always `--user-data-dir D:\fyx-user-check\profile-w1`, cwd in this checkout.
